About Whisk&Brew
Behind W&B
Whisk & Brew (W&B) is an independently conceived, designed, and developed digital café concept created as an academic case study. The project serves as an exploration of contemporary user interface design, focusing heavily on robust information architecture, structured data delivery, and cohesive digital brand systems.
The Concept
W&B functions as an informative, consumer-facing digital café platform engineered to help visitors effortlessly discover and compare regional café favorites within a single interface. The system synthesizes a curated catalog of beverages and confectionery items alongside approximate local market price structures and direct, actionable pathways to trusted consumer platforms. Operating strictly as an independent, non-commercial UI/UX case study, W&B is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any third-party businesses or establishments featured within the project.
W&B is designed to provide a warm, engaging, and informative café discovery experience.
